Your songs, settings and catalog are not touched by installing or removing this.
The one exception is the instrument-bank tick box: if you leave it on, installing
writes a one-line request there and KaraokeMachine deletes it once it has downloaded
the bank. They are under:

  ~/Library/Application Support/karaokemachine            settings.json, library.sqlite
  ~/Library/Application Support/karaokemachine/packages   your .kmpkg song packages
  ~/Library/Application Support/km-package-builder        the recent-folder list
  ~/Library/Application Support/km-remote                 catalog.sqlite, favorites.sqlite
  ~/Library/Application Support/km-admin                  downloaded banks and picture packs,
                                                          and any provider key you asked it to keep

Any .kmbuild file in a folder of songs is left alone too. Delete these by hand if you
want them gone.
