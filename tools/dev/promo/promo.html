KaraokeMachine

Pick a song by number, and sing.Pick a song by number, and sing.

Type a number, or point a phone at the code.
Each syllable fills in time with the music.

Any phone is the remote.

Search the songs, queue one, change the key. There is no app to install.

The queue says who sings next.

Five kinds of song file.

MIDI.kar .mid
Video.mp4 .mkv .webm
MP3+G.mp3 + .cdg
UltraStar.txt + .mp3
LRC.lrc + .mp3
