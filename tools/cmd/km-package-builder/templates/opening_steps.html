The mark is drawn by the class, so the list carries no characters of its own. **A rung is skipped by being passed.** An open names only the rungs it takes — which of them it takes is decided by `if`s with no page in reach — so a rung nothing reported is one there was nothing to do. **Which is why the dash means two things, told apart by where it sits.** Before the rung a failure stopped at, a skipped rung is one the open climbed past and did not need; after it, it is one the open was never going to reach. `failed_at` is the position of the rung that failed, and `usize::MAX` where none did. Called from both of `open_progress.html`'s arms that have a job to draw: the one still running, and the one that failed, where the list is what says *which rung*. #}
